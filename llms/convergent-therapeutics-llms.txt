# Convergent Therapeutics

> Convergent Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company in Cambridge, Massachusetts, developing next-generation targeted radiotherapies for cancer. Founded in 2020 by Philip Kantoff, it builds alpha radioantibodies that pair a humanized monoclonal antibody against prostate-specific membrane antigen (PSMA) with the alpha emitter actinium-225. Its lead candidate CONV01-alpha (rosopatamab tetraxetan) is in the Phase 2 CONVERGE-01 trial in metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer, including patients previously treated with Lu-177 PSMA radioligand therapy. Convergent is a therapeutics developer and publishes no public developer API, SDK, or machine-readable API contract.
